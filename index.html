Formula Reference: Given vs. Must Memorise

Given on Official Formula Sheet (Page 2)

  • Area of a triangle: $A = \frac{1}{2}bh$
  • Circle: $A = \pi r^2$, $\quad C = 2\pi r$
  • Curved surface area of cylinder: $A = 2\pi r h$
  • Curved surface area of cone: $A = \pi r l$
  • Surface area of sphere: $A = 4\pi r^2$
  • Volume of prism / cylinder: $V = Al$, $\quad V = \pi r^2 h$
  • Volume of pyramid / cone: $V = \frac{1}{3}Ah$, $\quad V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$
  • Volume of sphere: $V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$
  • Quadratic formula: $x = \dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$
  • Sine rule: $\dfrac{a}{\sin A} = \dfrac{b}{\sin B} = \dfrac{c}{\sin C}$
  • Cosine rule: $a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc\cos A$
  • Area of triangle (trig): $A = \frac{1}{2}ab\sin C$

Must Memorise (Not Given)

  • Trapezium area: $A = \frac{1}{2}(a + b)h$
  • Compound interest: $\text{Value} = P\left(1 + \dfrac{r}{100}\right)^n$
  • Arc & Sector: $L = \frac{\theta}{360}(2\pi r)$, $\quad A = \frac{\theta}{360}(\pi r^2)$
  • Pythagoras & SOHCAHTOA: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$
  • Exact trig values: $\sin, \cos, \tan$ for $0^\circ, 30^\circ, 45^\circ, 60^\circ, 90^\circ$
  • Coordinate Geometry: $m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$, Midpoint $\left(\frac{x_1+x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1+y_2}{2}\right)$, $d = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}$, Perp: $m_1 m_2 = -1$
  • Polygons: Interior sum $= (n-2)\times 180^\circ$; Exterior sum $= 360^\circ$
  • Similarity: Length $k \implies$ Area $k^2 \implies$ Volume $k^3$
  • Circle Theorems: All angle & chord-symmetry theorems (incl. Alternate Segment)
  • Vector magnitude: $\left|\begin{pmatrix}x\\y\end{pmatrix}\right| = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$
