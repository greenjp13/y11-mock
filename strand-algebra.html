E2 Decision Tree: Picking the Right Algebraic Method

CIE 0580 Extended tests algebraic manipulation, functions (including domain & range), linear inequality regions, curve asymptotes, and differentiation.

1. Quadratics & Linear Inequality Regions

  • "Give your answer to 2 d.p. / 3 s.f." $\implies$ Use the Quadratic Formula (Paper 4).
  • "Write in the form $(x+p)^2 + q$" $\implies$ Complete the Square; turning point is $(-p, q)$.
  • 2D Linear Regions: Solid line for $\le$ or $\ge$; dashed line for $<$ or $>$. Test $(0,0)$ to check which side satisfies the inequality.

2. Functions, Domain & Range

  • Composite $fg(x)$: Work right to left—substitute the entire $g(x)$ into $f$'s $x$-slots.
  • Inverse $f^{-1}(x)$: Set $y = f(x)$, rearrange to make $x$ the subject, then swap $y$ back to $x$.
  • Range of a quadratic on $[a, b]$: Always check if the vertex lies inside $[a, b]$—the min/max often occurs at the vertex, not an endpoint!

3. Differentiation & Turning Points

  • Power rule: $\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}(ax^n) = anx^{n-1}$; constants differentiate to $0$.
  • Stationary / turning points: Set $\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = 0$, solve for $x$, then substitute back into the original $y$ for coordinates.
  • Nature: $\frac{\mathrm{d}^2y}{\mathrm{d}x^2} > 0 \implies$ minimum; $\frac{\mathrm{d}^2y}{\mathrm{d}x^2} < 0 \implies$ maximum.

4. Algebraic Fractions & Proportion

  • Expression ($\frac{a}{x} + \frac{b}{x+1}$): Keep the common denominator! Never multiply it away.
  • Equation ($\frac{a}{x} + \frac{b}{x+1} = 1$): Multiply every term by the common denominator to clear fractions.
  • Proportion: Direct $y = kx^n$; Inverse $y = \frac{k}{x^n}$. Always find $k$ first.

High-Frequency CIE Examiner Traps

  • Sign error in subtraction: In $\frac{3}{x-2} - \frac{2}{x+1}$, the $-2$ multiplies both terms of $(x-2)$, giving $-2x + 4$, NOT $-2x - 4$.
  • Illegal cancelling: You can NEVER cancel across a $+$ or $-$ sign (e.g. $\frac{x^2 + 3}{x^2} \ne 3$). Factorise first, then cancel entire brackets.
  • Quadratic formula division: The denominator $2a$ divides the entire numerator $-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}$, not just the square root.
