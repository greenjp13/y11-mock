E3 Decision Tree: Core Coordinate Formulas (Must Memorise)

None of the coordinate geometry formulas appear on the CIE 0580 Formula Sheet—commit these four tools to memory before the mock.

1. Gradient, Midpoint & Distance

  • Gradient: $m = \dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$ (keep the order consistent top & bottom).
  • Midpoint: $M = \left(\dfrac{x_1 + x_2}{2},\; \dfrac{y_1 + y_2}{2}\right)$ (average the coordinates).
  • Length: $d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$ (Pythagoras).

2. Parallel & Perpendicular Lines

  • First step: Rearrange into $y = mx + c$ so you can read off $m$ safely.
  • Parallel: $m_2 = m_1$.
  • Perpendicular: $m_1 m_2 = -1 \implies m_2 = -\dfrac{1}{m_1}$ (negative reciprocal: flip & change sign).

3. Perpendicular Bisector of $AB$

  • Step 1: Find the midpoint $M$ of $AB$.
  • Step 2: Find $m_{AB}$, then take the negative reciprocal $m_{\perp} = -\dfrac{1}{m_{AB}}$.
  • Step 3: Use $y - y_M = m_{\perp}(x - x_M)$ (use $M$, NOT $A$ or $B$!).

4. Intersections, Collinearity & Axes

  • Intersection of two lines: Solve simultaneously.
  • Axis intercepts: Crosses $x$-axis when $y = 0$; crosses $y$-axis when $x = 0$.
  • Collinear points $A, B, C$: Show $m_{AB} = m_{BC}$ (with shared point $B$).

High-Frequency CIE Examiner Traps

  • Reading $m$ before isolating $y$: For $2x + 4y = 9$, the gradient is NOT $2$ and NOT $-2$. Divide by $4$ first: $y = -\frac{1}{2}x + \frac{9}{4} \implies m = -\frac{1}{2}$.
  • Using an endpoint instead of the midpoint: A perpendicular bisector must pass through the midpoint of $AB$, never $A$ or $B$.
  • Required form $ax + by = c$ with integer coefficients: Multiply through by the denominator to clear fractions (e.g. $y = \frac{2}{3}x - 4 \implies 2x - 3y = 12$).
