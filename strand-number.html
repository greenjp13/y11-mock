E1 Decision Tree: Picking the Right Method Fast

1. Surds & Rationalising (Paper 2)

  • Single surd denominator $\frac{a}{\sqrt{b}}$: multiply top & bottom by $\sqrt{b}$.
  • Binomial denominator $\frac{a}{p \pm \sqrt{q}}$: multiply top & bottom by the conjugate $p \mp \sqrt{q}$ so the bottom becomes $p^2 - q$.
  • Always pull out the largest square factor first (e.g. $\sqrt{72} = \sqrt{36 \times 2} = 6\sqrt{2}$).

2. Fractional & Negative Indices (Paper 2)

  • Order of attack for $\left(\frac{a}{b}\right)^{-m/n}$: 1) Flip for the minus sign, 2) Root ($n$th root) to keep numbers small, 3) Power ($m$).
  • Solving $a^x = b$: rewrite both sides with the same prime base (e.g. $8 = 2^3$, $9 = 3^2$, $\frac{1}{16} = 2^{-4}$).

3. Upper & Lower Bounds (Paper 4)

  • Half the rounding unit: nearest $10 \implies \pm 5$; 1 d.p. $\implies \pm 0.05$.
  • Max of $A - B$ $= A_{\text{UB}} - B_{\text{LB}}$; Min of $A - B$ $= A_{\text{LB}} - B_{\text{UB}}$.
  • Max of $\frac{A}{B}$ $= \frac{A_{\text{UB}}}{B_{\text{LB}}}$; Min of $\frac{A}{B}$ $= \frac{A_{\text{LB}}}{B_{\text{UB}}}$.

4. Sets, Percentages & Recurring Decimals

  • Sets: $A \cup B$ (either/both), $A \cap B$ (both), $A'$ (not in $A$), $n(A)$ (count). Fill the intersection first!
  • Reverse percentage: Divide by the multiplier (e.g. $15\%$ off $\implies \div 0.85$).
  • Recurring decimals: Multiply by $10^k$ to align repeating digits directly after the decimal point, then subtract.

High-Frequency CIE Examiner Traps

  • Standard Form format: $0.4 \times 10^2$ or $32 \times 10^{-5}$ loses the final A1 mark. The coefficient $a$ MUST satisfy $1 \le a < 10$.
  • Bounds premature rounding: Keep full calculator precision when dividing bounds, and only round to 3 s.f. on the final answer line.
  • Compound Interest "interest earned" vs "total amount": Read carefully—if asked for the interest, subtract the original principal $P$ at the end.
